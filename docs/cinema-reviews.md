## What each review record preserves

- Cinema ID and source listing, platform/access method and observed date.
- Public author display name and star rating where visible.
- Review date and visit date separately, or the publisher’s unchanged relative date.
- A short English paraphrase, mentioned sala/format and historical cautions.
- A text fingerprint for directly fetched cards; no full review text.
- Original review URL remains null when the source does not expose one.

Source positions are one-based positions in the retrieved page, not stable platform review identifiers. Fingerprints assist duplicate detection within a source; cross-platform deduplication remains uncertain without original IDs. Do not add platform counts or interpret this selected sample as a statistical survey.
