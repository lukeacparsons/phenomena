# Research on the 15 cinema additions

Researched 19 November 2026. This extends the original eight profiles with thirteen Barcelona cinemas, Filmax Gran Via in L’Hospitalet, and Ateneu Barcelonès as a cultural screening venue. It does not assert that ingestion/deployment is complete.

**89 room entries; 54 with capacity evidence (25 published counts and 29 booking-layout counts); 11 screen measurements; 38 downloaded photos across 12 venues; 15 salas with identified interior photos.**

Of the room entries, 61 have observed identities and 28 are explicitly unmapped placeholders accounting for published complex totals. SOM’s active programme includes Sala 14 despite a twelve-room total. Placeholders must never be joined to showtimes as actual sala numbers.

Maps links below are constructed name/address searches, not verified Google Place-ID links. No new Google rating snapshots or complete review exports were obtained. Tripadvisor directory matches and six short review observations are retained with their limitations.

Published capacity can mean seats or total admission capacity. Booking counts cover every rendered position regardless of current availability; they may include wheelchair/flagged positions or omit closed areas. Neither is represented as a certified legal capacity.

## Remaining gaps

- Capacity evidence is still missing for 35 room entries. All 28 Mooby rooms now have mapped-position counts; Cinesa, most Filmax rooms and Maldà need stronger capacity evidence.
- Screen dimensions remain missing for 78 of the 89 new room entries. No full current room length × width × height was established. Filmoteca and Ateneu publish floor areas only.
- No downloaded venue photos were obtained for Cinesa Diagonal, Cinema Maldà or Filmax Gran Via. Many photos at other complexes cannot be assigned a sala number.
- Some opening dates and ownership chains remain unresolved; website legal entities and cinema brands do not prove building ownership.
- Filmoteca Chomón has conflicting official capacities (360/364). Its detailed hardware inventory is dated February 2016.
- Verdi’s proposed additional rooms remain outside the verified nine-room roster.
