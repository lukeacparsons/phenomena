## Highest-value ways to fill the remaining gaps

1. **Ask for the current technical pack.** The cinema’s hire, events or projection team can often supply a sala list, seating plan, screen measurements, projector/audio inventory and labelled photos in one response.
2. **Follow public building records.** Licensing files, approved refurbishment drawings and accessibility reports can contain dimensions and seat schedules. Record the plan date and distinguish proposed, approved and completed work.
3. **Check installers and manufacturers.** Installation case studies can identify exact equipment and show labelled booth/sala photographs. Confirm later replacements before treating these as current.
4. **Use captions and room labels to identify photos.** Official 360 tours, event galleries and technical visits are stronger room evidence than an unlabelled review photo. Keep dates and reuse rights.
5. **Resolve conflicting prices and capacities explicitly.** Keep both sources, ask what each figure includes and get a date for the current configuration. Do not turn a booking-map position count into certified capacity.
