## Limits to check for any visit

<a id="question-seating"></a>
**Which seats should I choose?** A complete current seat-by-seat sightline, obstruction and legroom survey is not established; no exact “best seat” is asserted.

<a id="question-total_cost"></a>
**What will the visit really cost?** A final quote requires a specific screening, seat, ticket type, purchase channel and applicable extras. Published tariffs, memberships, hire fees and event prices are not interchangeable.

<a id="question-family"></a>
**Can I bring children or a baby?** Booster seats, pushchair storage, baby-changing facilities and a current sensory-session calendar are not fully documented. Film age classification is separate from the cinema’s ticket-age policy.

<a id="question-timing"></a>
**When should I arrive, and when will I leave?** Exact trailer duration and finish time are not established. Event-specific play periods, intervals, introductions or discussions must not be generalized to ordinary screenings. Parking hours are not auditorium hours.

<a id="question-language"></a>
**What language will I hear and read?** Confirm audio language, subtitle language and accessibility features for the exact screening. Original audio does not necessarily mean English; ordinary subtitles do not establish captions for deaf/hard-of-hearing viewers.

<a id="question-food_belongings"></a>
**Can I bring food, water or luggage?** Outside-food sourcing, water exceptions, bag storage, current menus and local allergen controls are not all established. Policies below are operator statements, not legal conclusions.

<a id="question-accessibility"></a>
**Can I comfortably access this particular sala?** A complete route and facility survey for every sala is not established. Check the room, lift limits, wheelchair/companion allocation, toilet route and required hearing/visual assistance.

<a id="question-booking_help"></a>
**What if my plans change or something goes wrong?** Transfer rules, partial-refund exceptions and current response times are not fully documented. A published contact does not guarantee a refund or an immediate reply.

Operator policies are statements, not legal conclusions. Date-specific prices and festival services are not regular cinema tariffs or recurring promises. No complete sightline/legroom survey or universal trailer duration is established.

**Texas accessibility correction retained:** its basement cinemas use a stairlift with a 150 kg combined person/wheelchair limit and no alternative route above it; the theatre entrance is separate. [Espai Texas — practical information](https://espaitexas.cat/informacio-practica/)
