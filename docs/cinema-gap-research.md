## Method and scope

- Unknown does not mean absent.
- Cultural venues host screenings but are not assumed to be daily cinemas.
- Event maxima, standing capacity, old launch capacity, and complex totals remain distinct from current sala seats.
- Only source-named rooms create records; festival sala numbers are flagged when permanent correspondence is unverified.
- Screen/display dimensions, projection throw, floor area and stage sizes remain separate.
- Maps URLs are name/address searches, not verified Place IDs.
- No review export, native-browser access, booking, purchase or venue contact.

Technical PDF pages were visually checked to distinguish area icons, seated counts, stage measurements and screen dimensions. Sources retain access method and checked date. Older observations and unavailable primary pages remain labelled.
