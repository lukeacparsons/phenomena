## Source conflicts

- **Yelmo Baricentro — room count:** Dated 2025 Yelmo list says 8; host-centre and ticket distributor say 11. Neither is silently selected as verified current. [Entradas Baricentro cinema information](https://cine.entradas.com/cine/barbera-del-valles/yelmo-cines-baricentro/informacion) · [Baricentro live public tenant data](https://cms.baricentro.es/api/public/comercios) · [Yelmo venue/format list dated February 2025](https://www.myentrada.com/imgs/YELMO_CINES-RELACI%C3%93N_2025-02.pdf)
- **Cines Imperial — ambiguous capacity:** Academy page describes an eleven-screen multiplex but also gives “current capacity 350” without identifying the room or scope. Do not use 350 as the complex total or assign it to Sala 1. [Acadèmia del Cinema Català: Imperial history and archive](https://academiadelcinema.cat/ca/cinemes-imperial-sabadell)
- **Kinépolis Mataró Parc — largest room capacity:** Sala 4 table gives 469 total butacas and 2 PMR; hire page gives maximum 467 seats. Difference may relate to accessible positions but source does not define this. [Kinépolis Mataró official sala table](https://kinepolis.es/cines/kinepolis-mataro-parc/info/) · [Kinépolis Mataró corporate-event facilities](https://empresas.kinepolis.es/cines/kinepolis-mataro/)
- **Cinema Edison — opening year:** Operator hire PDF says inaugurated 2018; operator spaces webpage says 2019. [Cinema Edison hire dossier, pages 2–6 and 10](https://cinemaedison.cat/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Lloguers-Edison-BO.pdf) · [Cinema Edison spaces](https://cinemaedison.cat/espais-edison/)

## Method

- Unknown means not established, not absent.
- Complex seat totals are never assigned to individual salas.
- Published screen pairs with unlabelled axes retain raw order and flag width/height inference.
- Stage dimensions and auxiliary projection screens are separate from main cinema screen dimensions.
- Dated source claims, conflicting capacities, and announcements remain labelled.
- Only source-identified room numbers or names create room records; complex totals alone do not create invented numbered rooms.
- Google Maps links are constructed name/address searches, not verified Place IDs.
- No native-browser, authenticated Maps, review export, ticket purchase or seat reservation was used.

72 sources were retained with access method and checked date. Search-index evidence is distinguished from directly fetched HTML/PDFs; source availability is not evidence that every claim is current. Publication rights for photos remain unestablished.
