# Regular-screening audit — 19 November 2026

Integration update: the nine nearby cinemas and six cultural programmes identified here are now included. See [live integration and coverage](nearby-cultural-integration.md): twelve sources currently supply verified original-audio sessions, while CCCB/Xcèntric, Albareda and Can Carol remain programme-link monitors. The findings below preserve the audit's pre-integration scope.

Follow-up to the existing Barcelona inventory: 16 cultural leads (including already-listed Ateneu), 23 civic locations, nine unintegrated nearby cinemas, 12 regional leads and three seasonal programmes. Programme records are not necessarily distinct physical venues. The 22 integrated cinema directory entries were already verified by live catalogue collection.

Official operator programmes, public booking catalogues/APIs, and municipal agendas. Regularity describes screening activity, not guaranteed original-language availability or exact opening days. No tickets or reservations were made.

## What to integrate next

The clearest additional Barcelona sources are Institut Français, CCCB/Xcèntric, CaixaForum Barcelona, La Casa Elizalde, Albareda and Can Carol. Palau Macaya has a verified four-Friday film cycle this month, including an explicitly VOSE screening. These deserve event-aware collectors, with every screening’s venue, date and version checked.

The 23 Pantalla locations are worth one shared programme collector. The published season contains 165 screenings from October 2026 to March 2027; it does not imply that every centre screens weekly. Map the actual room: Urgell can screen at IES Poeta Maragall and La Marina at Sala Maremar.

All 21 remaining nearby/regional cinema leads have evidence of active recurring film programmes. CineBaix and Cinema Edison are especially useful for original-language coverage; Cinesa, Yelmo and Kinépolis locations offer reuse of existing collectors. Sant Cugat Cinemas and Can Castellet still need more robust direct date extraction.

Original Spanish and Catalan qualify. VOSE/VOSC/VOSI/VO qualify with source evidence; a bare VE/VC or language name does not. Regular screenings and original-language screenings are separate questions.
