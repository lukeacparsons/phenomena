# Nearby and regional venue photos

18 photos plus one historical drawing across eight venues. Visually checked on 2026-11-19. These are real source images; capture dates are often unknown. Seven venues have non-predecessor photography, and four named cinema auditoriums have identified interior views.

[Research report](cinema-regional-research.md) · [Structured metadata](auditorium-images.json)

Imperial archive photographs are historical. Generic IMAX marketing artwork, corrupted downloads and duplicate thumbnails were excluded. Publication rights have not been established.
